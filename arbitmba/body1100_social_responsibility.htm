Project WHY !

Have you ever wondered what one person + another + another + another could do..
      * Keep 500 kids from falling out of the education net
      * Give 30 jobs to people no one believed in
      * Make 20 kids and even their family smile
      * Get many children the heartFix they need

Project WHY resource center is located in the Giri Nagar slum in New Delhi. There are currently 600 children enrolled at WHY. The project aims at providing the children with the means to learn earn and live with dignity. Children at WHY are enrolled in local government or municipal schools. Its goals are to :-
      * Evolve a sustainable child support system for deprived children
      * Ensure that all children attend school regularly
      * Help develop life skills
      * Empower the community to play an active role in education
      * Generate community resources to sustain the activities of the centre
      * Build confidence and competence
      * For more information on project WHY, please visit the website.

The entire Arbit Team (Arbit, his friends and his Creators) is extending a helping hand to the under-privileged children by announcing their support to Project WHY. We would appreciate all the Arbit fans who would come forward and help such initiatives.
