Arbit Choudhury was conceived during the heady days of MastishK 2004 - India's First fully online B-school event. MastishK was conceived by the students of NITIE as a platform for B-schoolers and corporate alike to battle through Business Simulation Games. MastishK was organized under the aegis of NITIE's Flagship event Prerana. Presented below a breif about NITIE, Prerana and MastishK

NITIE, Mumbai

National Institute of Industrial Engineering (NITIE) was established in 1963 by Government of India with the United Nations Development Program (UNDP) and International Labor Organization (ILO). NITIE has progressed to reach its current status as one of the pantheons of management institutions in India. The single minded dedication and focus with which the institute functions has culminated in managers par excellence who leave the hallowed grounds of this institute each year. Based on the fifteenth course plan of the Sloan Business School, the syllabus provides students an international benchmark/touchstone to mete their learning.

Prerana - Inspiring Solutions

Consistently ranked number one in extra curricular achievements, it is not a big surprise that NITIE’s events are looked forward to with great expectations from all corners, be it the corporate or the B-Schools. NITIE is the first B-School in India to launch a Summer Project Contest that has now evolved into one of the biggest national B-School events- Prerana. In fact NITIE has now become a podium for elite students from all over the country to prove their mettle.

The event, conceptualized by the pioneer PGDIM batch in 1995 encompasses a gamut of activities ranging from the deliberations on current issues amongst the doyens to toasting the best summer projects across the country to hosting a plethora of Business Challenge Games. Prerana includes traditional favorites like:- Prerana Business Meet – A confluence of Industrial barons deliberating and presenting their views on a contemporary topic. Summer Project Contest – India’s first summer project contest- Watch the country’s best summer interns showcase their caliber. Prerana Business Quiz – A virtual battle ground for the gray cells pitting the corporate against their B-school protégés. Srijan – A string of strategy games designed to test the skill set of soon-to-be managers. Red Herrings – An array of on-the-spot business games that gauge the participant's acumen in a diverse cross section of managerial functions.

MastishK - The Battle of Minds

The internet has truly revolutionized the modus operandi of Business throughout the world. Acknowledging the fact and nurturing the desire to establish a link between the intelligentsia in different B schools of the country, NITIEans, ascended to cyberspace with the Mind-boggling Online event - "MastishK". MastishK 2004 the first fully online gaming event amongst the Business schools in India was a huge success. Ever since the fruition of the event last year the only constant attribute of MastishK has been its zooming appeal amongst the student fraternity not only throughout the country but also abroad. At the same time the event has also been a crowd puller in terms of corporate participation from some of the bellwethers in the Indian Business arena.
