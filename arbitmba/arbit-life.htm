Detailed life history of Arbit Choudhury

Arpit Choudhury has born of 5th September 1980, in a mid-sized town called Buildingspur somewhere in central India. He was always brilliant academically, intellect was something he inherited from his parents, both of whom were successful professionals. But unlike most toppers, he never burnt the midnight oil preparing for exams. His motto was- “Study smart, not hard!!” He made sure that he only studied just enough to fill 2 to 3 sheets in the answer paper. Not more, not less. Understanding the subject was never on his agenda. But getting good marks was. So, he concentrated on “what”, rather than “why”. Armed with excellent cramming abilities, a good handwriting and super time management abilities, he was an unstoppable force in school. Arpit was a guy who eat cricket, slept cricket, lived cricket. He was the type of guy who would even watch English County Cricket on TV, and would rattle off cricket stats like no one else.

Arpit always felt that he very good in maths. Moreover, he hated biology. So, engineering was a natural choice for him, although his parents wanted him to study medicine. He knew that he was not good enough to crack IIT-JEE, and decided instead to channelize his energies towards the regional pre-engineering tests. He finally made it to a run-of-the-mill engineering college. During engineering, he realized, nothing had changed. He was still not interested in understanding any subject. But he could still top in class by cramming up the executive summaries of chapters. Life was bliss. He had everything. Everything except a girlfriend. No matter how hard he tried, he could never impress anyone from the opposite sex. Finally, in 3rd year, his luck changed, when one of his batchmates, whom he had been eyeing for ages, herself proposed to Arpit. As expected, he was bowled over by this, and thus began his long awaited love life.

Something else happened during 3rd year of Arpit’s engineering. He began to seriously think about his future. About what he would do beyond his B.E. Being one who was never interested in technical stuff, he knew that he would never enjoy working as an engineer. Nor did an M Tech. or M.S. degree enthuse him. He wanted pursue a career which required minimum knowledge, maximum street smartness, minimum hardwork, maximum returns, minimum substance, maximum “fart”. He soon realized, management was the way to go. He wanted to be an MBA. He had to crack CAT. Hence, for the 1st time in his life, Arpit began to study hard, with a single point agenda- making it to one of the top B-schools in the country. His efforts paid off spectacularly again. He made it to his dream B-school. And then began the most entertaining phase of his life. A phase of his life which this comic strip is all about…
