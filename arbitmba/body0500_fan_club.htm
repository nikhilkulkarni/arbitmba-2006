The Arbit Choudhury Fan Club

The Arbit Choudhury Fan Club is a virtual network of ALL who like Arbit.

An effort to reach out and remain connected ! An effort to ensure that the fans do not not miss out on any of the regular fortnightly Comic Strips nor any Arbit Updates. The Arbit Choudhury Website and Mailing List/ Yahoo group are the early initiatives in this direction.

Join the Arbit Mailing List/ Yahoo Group to receive fortnightly edition of the comic strip and Arbit Updates. To join the group, simply click here OR paste the following URL in the address bar of your Internet Browser and then click on "Join this group" button. http://groups.yahoo.com/group/arbit_mba/

The Fan Club also offers a chance for fans to send in their suggestions for making Arbit even more popular, and also ideas for the comic strips. The ideas that are found suitable for comic strips will be used to publish a comic strip and it will carry the name and institute/company etc of the contributor.
